Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you perform at?

I perform at corporate events, weddings, private parties, and special occasions throughout Seattle and the surrounding area.

How far in advance should I book?

It’s best to book as early as possible, especially for weekends and holidays. However, I can sometimes accommodate last-minute requests.

Do you travel outside Seattle?

Yes! I am available for events in the greater Seattle area and occasionally travel for special bookings.

Are your performances family-friendly?

My magic is suitable for all ages and can be tailored for both family and corporate audiences, but I do not offer shows specifically catered for children.
