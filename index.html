Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events do you perform at?

I perform at corporate events, weddings, private parties, and special occasions throughout Seattle and the surrounding area. I specifically offer 1–3 hour strolling magic where I mingle and perform close-up magic for small groups at a time, as well as 1 hour parlor sit-down shows that provide a more theatrical, interactive experience for your guests. For strolling gigs, I also love to offer an optional 5–10 minute finale-esque set that ties the whole room together—it's interactive and provides an unforgettable moment for everyone to enjoy at the same time! While not required, it's a fantastic way to end the night, and highlight whoever is being celebrated, whether it's someone's birthday, newly-weds, or a team celebration! All these options can be tailored to fit the unique needs of your event.

How far in advance should I book?

It’s best to book as early as possible, especially for weekends and holidays. However, I can sometimes accommodate last-minute requests. Check availability now!

Do you travel outside Seattle?

Yes! I am available for events in the greater Seattle area and occasionally travel outside Washington for special bookings.

Are your performances family-friendly?

My magic is suitable for all ages and can be tailored for both family and corporate audiences, but I do not offer shows specifically catered for children.
