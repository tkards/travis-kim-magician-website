Biography

Short Bio (50 words)

Travis Kim is a Seattle-based close-up magician specializing in world-class sleight-of-hand. As the resident magician at Haunted Soiree Seattle and headliner at The Magic Castle's Close-Up Gallery, Travis delivers unforgettable interactive magic experiences for corporate events, weddings, and private parties.

Long Bio (150 words)

Travis Kim is one of Seattle's premier close-up magicians, known for his exceptional sleight-of-hand artistry and engaging performance style. With a focus on intimate, interactive magic, Travis creates moments of genuine wonder that leave audiences talking long after the event ends.

As the resident magician at Haunted Soiree Seattle and a featured headliner at the prestigious Magic Castle's Close-Up Gallery in Hollywood, Travis has earned a reputation for excellence in the magic community. His performances blend technical mastery with personable charm, making him a sought-after entertainer for corporate events, weddings, holiday parties, and private gatherings.

Travis's clients include Microsoft, Magic 2 Magicians, Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center, and Card Kingdom. His 5-star ratings and enthusiastic testimonials reflect his commitment to creating memorable, magical experiences that perfectly complement any event. Based in Seattle, Travis performs throughout the Pacific Northwest and beyond.
